    Talk connection

    Cassini Live joins calls through Talk's high-performance backend (HPB) and needs that server's internal secret: internalsecret under [clients] in the signalling server's configuration, not the shared secret in Talk's settings. On Nextcloud All-in-One, print it with docker exec nextcloud-aio-talk printenv INTERNAL_SECRET.

    Captions stay off until the internal secret is saved here.
